Food safety

Your product touches our plastic first

Water, laban, juice, tahini, oil — when the container touches food, the factory has to be run like a food business. Ours is.

Certified

Three approvals behind every food container

  • ISO 22000

    Food-safety management

    An international standard for organisations in the food chain — including the people who make its packaging.

  • HACCP

    Hazard analysis & critical control points

    Risks are identified at each step of production and controlled before they reach your product.

  • JFDA

    Jordan Food & Drug Administration

    Our food-contact packaging is approved by the national food and drug authority.

What it means for you

Easier audits, fewer questions from your buyers

  • Packaging you can name with confidence in your own food-safety file
  • A supplier that already speaks the language of HACCP audits
  • Certificates you can pass on to export buyers in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Iraq and Palestine
  • One standard across food, dairy, beverage and medical containers
