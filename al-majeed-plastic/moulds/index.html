Mould making

The part of a bottle you never see

Every container starts as a block of steel. We cut ours on a 2,000 m² mould-making base inside the plant — for our own lines and for bottle machines like yours.

Why it matters

Owning the mould means owning the outcome

  • Shorter launches

    A new shape goes from drawing to trial pieces without a queue at an outside workshop.

  • Your shape, exactly

    Your bottle is designed around your brand and filling line, not adapted from someone else’s.

  • Fixes on the spot

    Neck finish, wall thickness or base stability — adjusted by the team that built the mould.

  • Consistency

    The same mould, maintained in-house, means the 10,000th piece matches the first.

What we build

Moulds for both sides of the plant

  • Blow moulds For bottles, gallons and containers — HDPE and PET.
  • Injection moulds For PET preforms, jars and tubs.
  • Moulds for your machines Already run your own blow-moulder? We make moulds for bottle machines too.
